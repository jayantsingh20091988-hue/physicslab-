THE IDEA

Physics should feel explorable.

A formula is not the beginning of physics. It is the language we use to describe what happens in the real world.

Physics Lab is designed around that idea. Instead of stopping at definitions and equations, each topic can grow into a complete learning experience:

THE PERSON BEHIND THE PROJECT

Built by Navneet.

Physics Lab is an independent student-built project created with a simple idea: physics should be something you can explore, not just something you memorize before an exam.

The project is being developed step by step, with interactive experiments, simulations, mathematical explanations and visual learning experiences being added over time.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

Is Physics Lab free? +

The goal of Physics Lab is to provide accessible interactive physics learning without putting core learning experiences behind a paywall.

Which classes are supported? +

Physics Lab is being structured around Classes 9, 10 and 11, with chapters being developed progressively.

Is this only for exam preparation? +

No. Exam preparation can benefit from understanding, but the larger goal is to connect concepts, mathematics, experiments, simulations and real-world physics.

Can I suggest a chapter? +

Yes. Use the feedback section and tell us which class and chapter you want next.
