THE BIG IDEA

What exactly is Mechanics?

⚙

The physics of motion and interaction

Mechanics is the part of physics that helps us understand how objects move, why their motion changes, how forces interact, how energy moves from one form to another, and how objects remain in stable motion.

Instead of simply memorising formulas, Physics Lab treats mechanics as a connected story:

Motion → Force → Energy → Gravity → Orbits → Space
01

Why does an object move?

We investigate motion and the forces responsible for changing it.

02

Where does the energy go?

We follow energy as it transfers between kinetic, potential and other forms.

03

Why doesn't Earth fly away?

Gravity, orbital motion and inertia connect the physics of Earth with the physics of space.

WHY THIS UNIT MATTERS

Mechanics is not just another physics unit.

It gives you a language for describing a huge part of the physical world.

When you understand mechanics, everyday events stop looking random. A moving bicycle, a swinging object, a satellite, a roller coaster, a falling stone and a rocket launch can all be analysed using connected physical ideas.
