START WITH A PROBLEM

Why did we need these ideas?

🧱

A force can change motion

Push a box and it may start moving. Push harder and its motion can change more quickly.

But how do we measure the effect?
⚡

Something is being transferred

When a force moves an object, something useful has happened. Physics needed a way to describe this transfer.

This leads us toward Work and Energy.
⏱️

Time also matters

Two machines may perform the same amount of work, but one may do it much faster.

This leads us toward Power.
💭

Think before learning the formula

Suppose two people move the same box through the same distance. If both exert the same force, should physics describe their work as different?

Probably not — and this gives us a clue about what a useful definition of work should contain.

02

THE FIRST IDEA

Work

IN SIMPLE WORDS

Work happens when a force causes displacement.

If you push an object but it does not move, the situation is different from pushing it and actually moving it through a distance.

Force + Displacement → Work
HOW DID THE IDEA DEVELOP?

Start with the simplest case

If force and displacement are in the same direction, a natural measure is:

W = F × s
W Work
F Force
s Displacement
BUT REAL LIFE IS NOT ALWAYS STRAIGHT

What if the force is at an angle?

Only the part of the force acting along the direction of displacement contributes to the work.

That is why the angle θ becomes important.

W = F × s × cosθ
↗

Positive Work

Force has a component in the direction of motion.

W > 0
↔

Zero Work

No displacement, or force is perpendicular to displacement.

W = 0
↙

Negative Work

Force has a component opposite to the motion.

W < 0

04

THE BIGGER IDEA

Energy

Why invent another quantity after Work?

Work describes an interaction and transfer. But physics also needed a quantity that describes the ability of a system to produce change.

Energy = ability to cause change / do work
🏃

Kinetic

Energy associated with motion.

🏔️

Potential

Energy associated with position or configuration.

🔥

Thermal

Energy associated with microscopic motion.

⚡

Electrical

Energy transferred through electrical systems.

ASK A SIMPLE QUESTION

Mass

A heavier object generally requires more work to change its motion.

STEP-BY-STEP DERIVATION

How do we reach ½mv²?

01

Start with Work

W = F × s
02

Use Newton's second law

F = ma
03

Use the motion relation

v² − u² = 2as
04

For an object starting from rest

u = 0
05

Therefore

KE = ½mv²

The ½ is not arbitrary. It emerges from the mathematics connecting force, acceleration and displacement.

HOW FAST?

Power

Why isn't Work enough?

Imagine two machines both doing 1000 J of work.

Machine A 1000 J / 10 s
Machine B 1000 J / 100 s

Same work. Different time. We therefore need another quantity.

P = W / t

Power tells us how quickly work is done.

POWER 100 W
🐎

Why do we hear about horsepower?

Before modern electrical power units became common, comparing machine output with familiar working animals was useful in industry.

Today the SI unit of power is the watt.

13

CLEAR THE CONFUSION

Common Misconceptions

✕

"If I use force, work must happen."

Not necessarily. Displacement is also required.

✕

"Zero work means nothing happened."

Zero work only refers to the net work associated with that force and displacement.

✕

"Energy gets destroyed."

Energy can be transferred or transformed.

✕

"More work always means more power."

Power also depends on how much time the work takes.

⚡

YOU BUILT THE LOGIC

Work → Energy → Power

Force acting through displacement gives us a way to describe work. Work is connected to changes in energy. Energy tells us about the ability of a system to produce change. Power tells us how quickly work is done.

W = F s cosθ KE = ½mv² PE = mgh Wnet = ΔKE ME = KE + PE P = W/t
